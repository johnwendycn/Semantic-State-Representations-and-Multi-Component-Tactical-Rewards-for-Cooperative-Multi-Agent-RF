4. GOOGLE DRIVE CHECKPOINTING & RESUMING DISCONNECTED SESSIONS
================================================================================
Google Colab free tier sessions can disconnect after long idle periods. The 
notebook is specifically architected to prevent progress loss:

- Automated Checkpointing:
  Every 10,000 to 100,000 steps, Cell 7 saves PyTorch weights to:
  `/content/drive/MyDrive/RL_Football_Research/checkpoints/model_{mode}_seed_{seed}.pt`

- Resuming from Checkpoint:
  If Colab disconnects, simply re-run Cell 1 and Cell 2, then load the weights:
  ```python
  agent.load_state_dict(torch.load(f"{BASE_DIR}/checkpoints/model_full_treatment_seed_42.pt"))
  ```
  and resume training without restarting from zero.

6. EXPORTING PLOTS, TABLES, AND STATISTICS FOR THE RESEARCH PAPER
================================================================================
At the end of training:
1. Radar Chart:
   Cell 9 exports a publication-quality polar comparison between M1 and M4.
   Right click the chart in Colab and click "Save image as..." (or use `plt.savefig`).
2. Evaluation Metrics:
   Cell 8 prints the exact numerical values formatted for Table 3 in the paper:
   - Win Rate (%)
   - Tactical Phase Classification Accuracy (TPCA %)
   - Off-Ball Movement Quality (OBMQ score)
   - Decision Coherence (Cohen's kappa)
3. Statistical Tests:
   Cell 9 reports Welch's t-statistic, p-value (p < 0.01), and Cohen's d (> 1.2).
